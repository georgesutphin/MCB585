Welcome to the MCB 585 R materials site! This site hosts the R programming and biostatistics lessons for [MCB 585](https://mcb.arizona.edu/), offered by the Department of Molecular & Cellular Biology at the University of Arizona.

These pages introduce R and RStudio and walk through core skills for quantitative biology in R: data types and structures, data frames and indexing, data manipulation and plotting, decision-making and loops, distributions and normality, hypothesis testing, survival analysis, power analysis, and the course final project. Students are not expected to have prior programming experience.

For details on the rest of MCB 585—including the full schedule, policies, and other course content—please see the main course website on D2L and the course syllabus.

**Learning Goals for These Materials:**

Students working through these lessons will be able to:
1.	Use the R programming environment to approach basic problems in biological sciences.
2.	Apply basic biostatistics methods, such as selecting and employing an appropriate statistical test and conducting a power analysis based on preliminary data.
3.	Access, manipulate, visualize, and analyze biological datasets in R.
4.	Use R to explore distributions, perform hypothesis tests (including survival analysis), and design experiments with appropriate power.

**Course Requirements:** See the D2L course site and the course syllabus for current requirements, expectations, and grading details.
